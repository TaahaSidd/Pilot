100% Terminal-Driven Privacy

No configuration files, no plain-text .env file footprint left on your drive. Enter your Groq token and portal credentials straight into the secure command console prompt on launch. Your keys remain completely private to your local runtime session.

Ultra-Fast Quiz Resolution

Backed by Groq's blindingly fast Llama 3.3 70B model execution framework. Pilot uses your standard reading materials on screen as temporary context stores to answer complex multi-choice assignments accurately—safely halting submission if formatting anomalies arise.
